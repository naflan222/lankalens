LankaLens Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This policy applies to paid digital promotion services purchased on LankaLens, such as listing boosts, featured placements and promotional badges.

Failed or cancelled payments

If a payment fails or is cancelled before it is confirmed as successful, the promotion will not be activated and LankaLens will not treat the order as paid.

Activated promotions

Once a promotion has been successfully activated and delivery of the digital promotion service has started, the purchase is generally non-refundable.

Technical problems

If payment succeeds but LankaLens cannot provide the purchased promotion because of a technical problem on our side, contact us. We may restore the full promotion period, provide an equivalent promotion, or issue a refund where appropriate.

Duplicate or incorrect charges

If you believe you were charged more than once for the same promotion, or the amount charged does not match the order, contact us with the order or transaction reference. We will review the records and correct any confirmed duplicate or incorrect charge.

Rule violations

Paid promotion does not exempt a listing from LankaLens rules. Listings may still be removed, paused or restricted if they violate our Terms & Conditions or applicable requirements. A refund is not automatically due where a promotion is interrupted because of a user's breach of those rules.

Refund requests

Email support@lankalens.online with your LankaLens account email, listing title, order or PayHere reference and a short description of the issue. Do not send card numbers, PINs, OTPs or banking passwords.

Processing

Where a refund is approved, it will be processed through the applicable payment method or provider. The time for funds to appear may depend on PayHere, the card issuer or the bank.
